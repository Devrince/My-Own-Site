# My-Own-Site
Making my own Site where u can post about myself, my projects, and my Socials for the connections!

I made this so i have a simple place on the web.

## Files
- index.html is the blog
- socials.html has my links
- projects.html has my projects
- about.html is about me
- styles.css has the colors and layout
- script.js shows the posts on the blog
- posts.js is where the posts are
- logo.png is the logo

## How I made it
- I made a folder called My-Own-Site and opened it in VS Code.
- I made the pages one by one: index, socials, projects and about.
- I made styles.css for the colors and the look.
- I made the logo from Gemini.
- I added my posts in posts.js.
- I opened the site in the browser and checked every page.
- I pushed it to GitHub and turned on Pages.
